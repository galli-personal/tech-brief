Source: BleepingComputer
📄 Exec summary

Commodity infostealers (Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine, Acreed on Windows; Atomic Stealer on macOS) picked up on users' own machines are stealing authenticated Claude browser sessions, letting attackers bypass passwords and 2FA entirely. Source: BleepingComputer
📄 Exec summary
