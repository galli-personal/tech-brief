All FIPS 140-2 cryptographic module validations move to Historical status

As of 21 September 2026, remaining FIPS 140-2 certificates have moved to Historical status under NIST's Cryptographic Module Validation Program; FIPS 140-3 is now the only standard accepted for new validations, though existing Historical-status modules remain usable subject to agency or contract terms.

Source: NIST CSRC
📄 Exec summary

Action: if you sell into government, defence or regulated clients, audit which products in your stack still cite FIPS 140-2 validation and confirm with vendors whether FIPS 140-3-validated versions exist for new procurements.
