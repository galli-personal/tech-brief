RMM abuse emerges as a top cybersecurity risk for MSPs

Use of legitimate remote monitoring and management tools by attackers is up 277% year-over-year, involved in 45% of endpoint incidents in early 2026. Attackers increasingly ride trusted tools, authenticated sessions and mailbox rules rather than novel exploits.

Source: ChannelE2E
📄 Exec summary
RMM tooling is the crown jewels of any MSP's stack precisely because it's trusted and has broad reach across client environments, which is exactly why attackers are pivoting to abuse it instead of building new malware.
