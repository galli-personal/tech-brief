CVE-2026-65660 (SharePoint, CVSS 8.8) was originally filed as a spoofing bug but turns out to allow remote code execution; exploitation attempts detected from 24 September. A chained MikroTik RouterOS exploit ("MikroTrick") grants full unauthenticated admin console access on vulnerable 7.x builds.

Source: The Hacker News
📄 Exec summary
Two lessons in one story: vendor severity classifications can be wrong (this was filed as "spoofing," not RCE), and router/network-gear vulnerabilities are just as exploitable as server-side ones.
